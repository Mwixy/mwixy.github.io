How we use your data

Your data is used only to provide the service, authenticate you, send notifications, enable moderation, and optionally collect deletion feedback.

We will never sell your data, use it for advertising, share it with third parties, or repost your content without your explicit permission.
4

Push notifications

Storii may send push notifications for likes, comments, friend requests, and alternate endings. To deliver these, we may store a device token provided by your OS.

Control which notifications you receive in Settings.
Disable all notifications in your device's system settings.
Device tokens are never shared with third parties.
5

Data storage & security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers in the European Union. Firebase provides TLS encryption in transit and encryption at rest.

Your password is handled entirely by Firebase Authentication — we never see, store, or have access to it. No system is 100% secure, but we take all reasonable measures to protect your data.

6

Data retention & deletion

Deleted posts and comments are immediately and permanently removed from our servers. We do not keep backups of deleted content.

When you delete your account, your profile, posts, and all associated data are permanently deleted. Any deletion reason you provided is stored anonymously only — it is not linked to your personal data after deletion. This action is irreversible.

We do not currently delete inactive accounts automatically. This policy may change with notice.

7

Children's privacy

Storii requires users to be at least 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Age is self-declared during account creation.

If you believe a child under 13 is using Storii, contact us at kiregekm@gmail.com and we will promptly delete their account.
